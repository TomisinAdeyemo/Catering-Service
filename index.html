ABOUT

Food, wine & quiet
moments worth remembering.

At Peschello Catering, we believe every event is an opportunity to create lasting memories through exceptional food. Rooted in rich African traditions and blended with contemporary culinary artistry, our dishes are crafted with fresh ingredients and authentic spices.

Chef-led and deeply seasonal, Peschello explores the relationship between the plate and the glass. Our menu changes with the market, while the room stays warm, understated and unhurried.
